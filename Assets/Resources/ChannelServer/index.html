Hover light & empty slots

Post to the Content Hub

Shares one of your themes, or the pictures and previews you made for a channel, under the account the TV is signed in to. Your phone doesn't need to sign in. It's marked [TV] so other TV owners can find it. Tip: switch to the theme on the TV first, so the TV can take a picture of it for the preview.

Disc games

Drop GameCube or Wii disc images here and they're copied to the TV's Games folder. They show up in the Disc Channel right away, with their real names read from the disc. Big files are fine: keep this page open until it says done.
